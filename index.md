## My Project

### Tourism and Consumer Prices in Macao (2010–2025)

**Research Question:** How are visitor arrivals associated with Macao's Composite Consumer Price Index (CPI) from 2010 to 2025?

**Project Overview:**

This project explores monthly visitor arrivals and consumer prices in Macao. It compares trends before, during, and after the COVID-19 disruption.

**Key Findings:**

Visitor arrivals dropped sharply during the COVID-19 period, while the Composite CPI remained relatively stable. The overall correlation between visitor arrivals and CPI was weak, suggesting that tourism alone cannot explain changes in consumer prices.
